How to test (student side)

  1. Download the Automatic Transmissions pack above (saves to your Downloads / Drive).
  2. Open Student · Fill. Click Browse for a file and pick the pack you just downloaded.
  3. Enter a name + vehicle, open a JOB, fill it in — confirm the button turns into Submit job sheet when complete.
  4. Try Save my work (Drive), then reopen that one file to confirm your pack + answers come back.
  5. Confirm it all works on a Chromebook under the normal student login (no console errors, downloads allowed).
